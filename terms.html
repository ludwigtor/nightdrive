NightDrive Terms of Service

Last updated: 15 November 2026

NightDrive is a private desktop tool that its owner uses to send short videos they have edited on their own computer to their own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

Use

NightDrive only acts on the TikTok account of the person who signs in and only does what that person starts: uploading a video they chose to their TikTok drafts. Nothing is posted publicly by NightDrive; the account owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app.

Content

The account owner is responsible for the videos they upload and for having the right to use them, including permission from any creator whose content appears in them, and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

No warranty

NightDrive is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app's settings.
