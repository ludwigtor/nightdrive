NightDrive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

NightDrive is a private desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It has no servers and no other users.

What it accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile (scope user.info.basic): your display name, to show which account is connected.
  • Upload to drafts (scope video.upload): to send a video you chose to your TikTok inbox as a draft.

What it stores

The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer, used only to perform uploads the owner starts, and deleted when the owner disconnects. No TikTok data is sold, shared, or sent anywhere other than TikTok's own API.

Your control

You can disconnect NightDrive in its settings, which deletes the stored tokens, and revoke its access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy.
